Method

Clear scope, controlled site work, durable handover.

  1. Scope the outcome. We clarify the required finish, programme, access, services and coordination risk before work starts.
  2. Coordinate the build. Site labour, trades and executive-level decisions stay connected so issues are solved early.
  3. Finish for use. The work is judged by the people who occupy, operate and maintain the space after handover.
